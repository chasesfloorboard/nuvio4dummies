Nuvio 4 Dummies

Set up Nuvio in four steps.

Netflix, Disney+, Hulu and seven more services, thirteen genres, and shows that actually play. Do this on the Android phone or tablet you'll watch on, or follow the TV guide.

Open this page on your phone. Scan the code with your camera. Every step here happens in the Nuvio app on that phone.

  1. Nuvio isn't in the Play Store. You install it straight from Nuvio's official download.

    Download Nuvio

    1. If Chrome warns "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway

    2. When it finishes, tap Open

      Missed it? Open your Files or Downloads app and tap the Nuvio file.

    3. If your phone says it can't install unknown apps

      Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

    4. Tap Install, then Open

      If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.

    5. Make your profile

      When Nuvio shows Who's watching? with nobody there, tap Manage Profiles, then Add Profile. Type any name and tap Create Profile at the bottom.

    Samsung phone says the app was blocked? Turn off Auto Blocker in Settings › Security and privacy, install Nuvio, then you can turn it back on.

    No account needed. When Nuvio asks you to sign in, you can tap Continue Without Account, or make an account so your setup follows you to other devices.

    Nuvio isn't available for iPhone or iPad right now. It isn't in the App Store, and its iPhone test version is closed.

    You can still use your iPhone to put Nuvio on your TV with a Fire TV Stick. Open the guide below.

    Nuvio is set up on an Android phone or tablet. Scan the code at the top with your phone, and this page opens there with a download button.

    Can't find Nuvio on your smart TV? Here's how to get it on a Firestick

    Roku can't run Nuvio, and the Samsung and LG versions need developer tools to install. The easy way is an Amazon Fire TV Stick plugged into your TV. These steps use the Fire TV remote.

    1. Install the Downloader app

      On the Fire TV home screen, go to Find (the magnifying glass), search Downloader and install the one with the orange icon.

      Downloader isn't there? Then this Fire TV can't install Nuvio. The newest Fire TV Stick 4K Select is one of these.

    2. Let Downloader install apps

      Settings›My Fire TV›Developer options›Install unknown apps

      Turn Downloader on.

      No Developer options? Go to Settings › My Fire TV › About, then press the middle button 7 times on the first line (your Fire TV’s name, like Fire TV Stick 4K) until it says you're a developer. Then go back and it will be there.

    3. Open Downloader and type this address

      /tv

      Press Go. If Downloader asks for access to files, choose Allow.

    4. Install Nuvio

      Wait for the download to finish, then choose Install and Open.

      Afterwards Downloader offers to delete the install file. Choose Delete to free up space; Nuvio stays installed.

    5. Open Nuvio

      Choose Continue without account. When it asks you to choose, pick Advanced, not Essential. Essential can't add your homepage.

    6. Set up your homepage

      Follow Setting up Nuvio on a TV at the bottom of this page. You do it from your phone, no typing on the remote.

    Google TV or Android TV instead (Chromecast, Sony, TCL, Hisense)? Get Downloader from the Play Store, and allow it under Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources. The rest is the same.

  2. One button copies everything and opens Nuvio. Then you paste it in.

    1. In Nuvio, go to

      Profile›Layout›Collections

      Profile is the last tab at the bottom of Nuvio.

    2. Tap the clipboard at the top right

      Collections
      0 collection(s), 0 folder(s)

      Not the two squares next to it. That one copies instead of pasting.

    3. Hold your finger in the box, tap Paste, then Import

      Import Collections

      Paste your collections JSON below.

      JSONPaste
      CancelImport

      Box still empty? Wait a second and tap Paste again.

    Importing replaces any collections already in this Nuvio profile.

    Rows say "Add a TMDB API key in Settings"? That Nuvio is an old or unofficial copy. Uninstall it, install it again with the Download Nuvio button in step 1, then do this step again.

  3. Nuvio needs the free Cinemeta addon to open movies and shows. Without it, tapping a title says "Failed to load".

    Nuvio opens and adds it. Then come back here.

  4. Your homepage shows what's out there. To press play, Nuvio needs a source: Real-Debrid (paid, a few dollars a month) and Comet (free, we set it up for you).

    1. Get Real-Debrid

      Make an account and buy a plan. Already have one? Skip this.

      Open Real-Debrid

    2. Copy your Real-Debrid key

      This opens your key page. Hold your finger on the long code under API private token, tap Select all, then Copy.

      Open my key page

      Page says 403-Forbidden? You're not logged in. Tap Login at the top, sign in, then tap the button above again.

    3. Paste it here

      Nuvio opens and adds Comet. Then come back here.

      Your key goes only to Comet, never to this website. Don't share it: it's your Real-Debrid account.

You're all set.

Open Nuvio, pick a service from your homepage and press play.

Setting up Nuvio on a TV

You set the TV up from your phone. Keep the phone and the TV on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. On the TV, go to

    Settings›Content & Discovery›Addons›Manage from phone

    No Collections tab on the phone in the next step? Nuvio is in Essential mode. On the TV, go to Settings › Advanced › Switch to Advanced, then open Manage from phone again.

  2. Scan the QR code on the TV with your phone's camera

    A Nuvio page opens on your phone.

  3. Add your homepage

    On that page, tap Collections, then Import, then URL. Paste this link and tap Import:

    /collections/homepage.json

    Then tap Save Changes at the bottom, and choose Confirm on the TV. Your Streaming Services and Genres rows appear on the TV's Home, below Nuvio's own rows.

  4. Add Comet

    Do step 4 on your phone first, up to a key that works. Then tap Copy addon link:

    On the Nuvio page, tap Addons, paste it under Add addon by URL and tap Add. Then Save Changes, and Confirm on the TV.
