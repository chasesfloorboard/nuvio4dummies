Nuvio 4 Dummies

Set up Nuvio in four steps.

Netflix, Disney+, Hulu and more on your homepage, and shows that actually play. Do this on the phone, tablet or computer you'll watch on.

You'll need a paid Real-Debrid plan, a few dollars a month. It's what plays the shows. Everything else here is free.

Watching on your phone instead? Scan the code with your camera and do these steps there. To watch on this computer, carry on here.

  1. Nuvio is free on Google Play.

    Get Nuvio on Google Play

    1. Tap Install, then Open

      Check it's Nuvio by Nuvio Media.

    2. Make your profile

      Tap Continue Without Account (or sign in). On Who's watching?, tap Manage Profiles, Add Profile, type a name and tap Create Profile.

    Didn't work?
    • Not in your Play Store, or it says it isn't available? Download Nuvio directly instead. Chrome will warn you; tap Download anyway, then Open, allow installs from this source if asked, then Install.
    • Play says Nuvio is already installed, or it won't update? You have a copy from somewhere else. Uninstall it, then install it from Google Play.
    • Samsung blocks the direct download? Turn off Auto Blocker in Settings › Security and privacy, install, then turn it back on.

    Nuvio isn't in the App Store. On iPhone and iPad you install it with AltStore, a free app for installing apps from outside the App Store. It takes about 20 minutes and a Windows PC or Mac, once.

    1. Put AltStore on your iPhone

      On your computer, open altstore.io and follow its steps for Windows or Mac. It installs AltStore on your iPhone over the cable.

      Open altstore.io

    2. Add Nuvio to AltStore

      Nothing happened? In AltStore tap Sources, then +, paste the link (it's already copied) and tap Add.

    3. Install Nuvio

      In AltStore, open Browse, find Nuvio and tap Free. Then open Nuvio from your home screen.

    4. Make your profile

      Tap Continue Without Account (or sign in). On Who's watching?, tap Manage Profiles, Add Profile, type a name and tap Create Profile.

    Every 7 days: open AltStore and tap Refresh All, with your computer on and AltServer running on the same Wi-Fi. If you skip it, Nuvio stops opening until you refresh.

    Didn't work?
    • iPhone says "Untrusted Developer"? Go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID and tap Trust.
    • AltStore or Nuvio won't open and asks for Developer Mode? Turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode and restart the iPhone.
    • Using SideStore instead of AltStore? .
    • Needs iOS 16.1 or newer.

    Nuvio for computers is free from Nuvio's own site on GitHub. It's an early test version: it works, but the phone app is smoother.

    Download for Windows Download for Mac

    Mac from before 2021 (Intel)? Download the Intel version instead.

    1. Windows: open the file you downloaded

      If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Click through the installer, then open Nuvio from the Start menu.

    2. Mac: open the file you downloaded

      Drag Nuvio into Applications, then open it. If it says it can't check Nuvio for malicious software, click Done, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

    3. Make your profile

      Click Continue Without Account (or sign in). On Who's watching?, click Manage Profiles, Add Profile, type a name and click Create Profile.

    Didn't work?
    • Download didn't start? Get it from Nuvio's releases page: the file ending .msi is for Windows, arm64.dmg for newer Macs and x86_64.dmg for Intel Macs.
    • Mac says Nuvio "is damaged"? You have the wrong version for your Mac. Try the other download.
    • Linux? The same page has AppImage, .deb, .rpm and Flatpak files.
    Can't find Nuvio on your smart TV? Here's how to get it on a Firestick

    Roku can't run Nuvio, and the Samsung and LG versions need developer tools to install. The easy way is an Amazon Fire TV Stick plugged into your TV. These steps use the Fire TV remote.

    1. Install the Downloader app

      On the Fire TV home screen, go to Find (the magnifying glass), search Downloader and install the one with the orange icon.

      Downloader isn't there? Then this Fire TV can't install Nuvio. The newest Fire TV Stick 4K Select is one of these.

    2. Let Downloader install apps

      Settings›My Fire TV›Developer options›Install unknown apps

      Turn Downloader on.

      No Developer options? Go to Settings › My Fire TV › About, then press the middle button 7 times on the first line (your Fire TV’s name, like Fire TV Stick 4K) until it says you're a developer. Then go back and it will be there.

    3. Open Downloader and type this address

      /tv

      Press Go. If Downloader asks for access to files, choose Allow.

    4. Install Nuvio

      Wait for the download to finish, then choose Install and Open.

      Afterwards Downloader offers to delete the install file. Choose Delete to free up space; Nuvio stays installed.

    5. Open Nuvio

      Choose Continue without account. When it asks you to choose, pick Advanced, not Essential. Essential can't add your homepage.

    6. Set up your homepage

      Follow Setting up Nuvio on a TV at the bottom of this page. You do it from your phone, no typing on the remote.

    Google TV or Android TV instead (Chromecast, Sony, TCL, Hisense)? Get Downloader from the Play Store, and allow it under Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources. The rest is the same.

  4. Cinemeta opens movies and shows. Comet finds them and plays them through your Real-Debrid.

    Tap each one; Nuvio opens and adds it. Come back here after each.

    Each button copies a link. After each one, go to Nuvio's Profile › Content & Discovery › Addons, click in the Addon URL box, press Ctrl+V and click Install Addon.

    Add Comet unlocks once your key works in step 3.

    Then check both are listed in Nuvio under Profile › Content & Discovery › Addons.

    Not in the list?
    • Close Nuvio all the way (swipe it away from your recent apps), open it again and check the list.
    • Still missing? Tap its button here again (that copies its link). Then on Nuvio's Addons page, hold your finger in the Addon URL box, tap Paste, then Install Addon.
    • Still missing? Click its button here again and paste it once more. If Nuvio says it's already installed, quit Nuvio and open it again.
    • Says "This profile uses primary addons"? Switch to your first profile and try again.

You're all set.

  1. Open Nuvio and pick a show

    Your streaming services are at the top of Home. TapClick one, then a show or movie.

  2. Tap PlayClick Play

    For a show, pick the episode you want instead.

  3. Pick the top one in the list

    Every one is the same episode, just a different copy. 2160p is 4K, 1080p is HD. If one won't play, go back and pick the next.

Something wrong?
  • "No streams" or "Playback unavailable"? Open Profile › Content & Discovery › Addons and check that both Cinemeta and Comet are there. If one is missing, do step 4 again, then close Nuvio and open it again.
  • Streaming services at the bottom of Home? You added them before this guide pinned them to the top. Do step 2 again; it moves them up.
  • Brand-new or rare titles can have nothing yet. Try again in a day or two.
Setting up Nuvio on a TV

You set the TV up from your phone. Keep the phone and the TV on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. On the TV, go to

    Settings›Content & Discovery›Addons›Manage from phone

    No Collections tab on the phone in the next step? Nuvio is in Essential mode. On the TV, go to Settings › Advanced › Switch to Advanced, then open Manage from phone again.

  2. Scan the QR code on the TV with your phone's camera

    A Nuvio page opens on your phone.

  3. Add your homepage

    On that page, tap Collections, then Import, then URL. Paste this link and tap Import:

    /collections/homepage.json

    Then tap Save Changes at the bottom, and choose Confirm on the TV. Your Streaming Services and Genres rows appear at the top of the TV's Home.

  4. Add Comet

    Do step 3 on your phone first, up to a key that works. Then tap Copy addon link:

    On the Nuvio page, tap Addons, paste it under Add addon by URL and tap Add. Then Save Changes, and Confirm on the TV.
